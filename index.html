01

Research

From a protein’s structure
to what it can do.

My research connects protein inverse folding with functional optimization. I am interested in how generative models can use structural constraints and activity signals to propose useful enzyme sequences.

I.

Structure-aware generation

Graph diffusion and inverse folding for sequence design conditioned on a protein backbone.

II.

Learning from function

Enzyme-domain adaptation, generative dataset curation, and KL-regularized GRPO.

III.

Design under constraints

Motif-preserving sequence redesign and computational analysis of protein interfaces.

RESEARCH ARTICLE2026 · JCIM

CatIF-RL: Activity-Oriented Enzyme Sequence Design by Steered Inverse Protein Folding

Yanheng Li, Jialong Xiong, Yuxin Zhang, Tong Cai, Chuan Fu, Shutong Li, Wei Xu, Ruoyi Lyu, Zhaoyang Chen, Zheng Guo, Xinqi Gong, Feng Wang.

Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling 66(15), 9031–9044.

CatIF-RL steers graph-diffusion inverse folding toward higher predicted catalytic activity through enzyme adaptation, generative dataset curation, and reinforcement learning.

Reading the result. The reported approximately 4× gain is in surrogate-predicted kcat relative to native enzymes, not experimentally measured enzyme activity. Sequence recovery is 0.55.
