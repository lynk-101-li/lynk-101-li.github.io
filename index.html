RESEARCH ARTICLE

CatIF-RL: Activity-Oriented Enzyme Sequence Design by Steered Inverse Protein Folding

Yanheng Li, Jialong Xiong, Yuxin Zhang, Tong Cai, Chuan Fu, Shutong Li, Wei Xu, Ruoyi Lyu, Zhaoyang Chen, Zheng Guo, Xinqi Gong, Feng Wang.

J. Chem. Inf. Model. 2026, 66(15), 9031–9044.

An approach to enzyme sequence design that brings together graph-diffusion inverse folding, enzyme adaptation, generative dataset curation, and reinforcement learning.

What the result means. The reported approximately 4× gain is in surrogate-predicted kcat relative to native enzymes, not experimentally measured enzyme activity. Sequence recovery is 0.55.
