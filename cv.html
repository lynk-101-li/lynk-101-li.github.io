Yanheng Li 李彦衡

Lynk · Biology & computational protein design

Beijing Institute of Technology

GitHub: lynk-101-liORCID: 0009-0009-3101-2673

Research interests

Structure-conditioned protein generation and activity-oriented enzyme sequence design. Methods include graph-diffusion inverse folding, enzyme-domain adaptation, generative dataset curation, and KL-regularized group relative policy optimization (GRPO).

Publications

First author. Published online July 17, 2026. Reported activity gains are surrogate predictions of kcat, not experimental measurements.
